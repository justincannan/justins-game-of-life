## Files
- `popdish/pop.py`: the population-dish prototype (numpy, CPU). Each square holds counts of active cells (A) and
  inactive cells (I), and sugar (n). Every division, inactivation and move is drawn binomially from those counts.
  Its arguments are sugar, softness, ticks and an optional seed.
- `popdish/sweep2.png`: its sweep of sugar 2 to 20 × softness 0 to 0.15, 4,000 ticks, one run each, on a 300 × 300 grid.
- `justins_game_of_life.html`: the current page. The Game of Life rule is off by default; the dish has a round rim,
  sugar, air, inactive cells and remains.
- `tools/`: the page's sources and its build script (`tools/patch.py`; see README.md for the build command).

## 3. The two missing regions (priority 2; only after 2 has some results)
These are proposals to try. Record each as a proposal, with its result.
- **DLA corner** (hard agar, poor sugar: thin branches). Try slower sugar diffusion (Dn 0.05 and 0.02) and a lower
  carrying capacity per square. DLA-like growth should appear when growth is limited by sugar diffusion.
- **Concentric rings** (Matsushita: the front alternately advances and rests). Do not script a clock. Look for a
  cycle that comes from the rules, for example movement switching on only above a local density of active cells,
  so the front waits while cells divide, then moves. Report whether rings appear, and their spacing.
