- Small patches: start a 10 × 10 block of the inoculum (the dish's own starting cells) at a fixed sugar level, in a
  plate at least 100 × 100 places across.
- Track each 10 × 10 block, which is one square of the population dish, over time. For each block and tick-window,
  record:
  - active and inactive cells, and sugar (en, summed over the block);

  - cells leaving the block per active cell per tick, against cell density and sugar, at gliding 0, 0.1 and 0.3
    (the page's nearest thing to agar softness).

  movement rule). Report the fitted constants, how well each form fits, and where it fails. If a form fits badly,
  propose a better one and note it as a proposal.
- Output: a JSON of fitted constants, plots of measured rate against sugar with the fitted curve, and a short summary.
