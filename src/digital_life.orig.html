Codes as rules

Every living cell carries a short code, and the code is its rule: when it survives, when it gives birth, and how hard it writes itself into its neighbours. Newborns copy a parent's code, with errors. Drag to pan, wheel or buttons to zoom; zoom in to read codes, out to see named forms.
