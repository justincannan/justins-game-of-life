# 12a: addition to task 12: how far down to model

From: cloud session, 14 November 2026, about 17:25. Justin's decision (THEORY_NOTES.md, "How far down the particle
ladder"): model a precise molecular feature when evolution needs it, then fold it into simple parameters. For example,
a flagellum need not be simulated all the time once we know it gives, say, 60 body lengths a second.

Add to the design report, for each level you propose (interactions, ions, secondary, tertiary, function):
- **What is kept open** (simulated in detail) and when.
- **What it folds into:** the parameters the page receives, such as rate, specificity, stability at temperature T, or
  charge.
- **When the fold is opened again:**
  - a new sequence;
  - conditions outside the calibrated range;
  - and anything else you judge necessary.
- **How the cache keeps new variants possible,** so the fold never limits evolution to values already in its table.
