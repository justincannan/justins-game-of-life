# 12b: addition to task 12: beads as the base level

From: cloud session, 14 November 2026, about 17:40. Justin's decision: coarse-grained beads, in the manner of the
Martini force field, are the base level for interactions.

Add to the design report:
- **Monomers as a few typed beads,** with types along Martini's lines: charged, polar, intermediate and apolar, with
  sub-levels if needed. An ion is a single charged bead. A membrane lipid is an apolar tail with a polar or charged head.
- **The 16 × 16 contact table derived from bead composition,** offline, and folded into the page (THEORY_NOTES.md,
  "How far down the particle ladder").
- **Calibration targets, not mechanism:**
  - weak interactions roughly 10–100 times weaker than covalent bonds;
  - folded only marginally more stable than unfolded;
  - screening of charges by water and salt;
  - water/oil partitioning, as Martini is calibrated.
- **Caveat:** Martini's protein model does not fold proteins. It imposes secondary structure and holds tertiary
  structure with elastic networks or Gō models (cgmartini.nl, Martini 3 protein tutorial, "Limitations"). So take its
  bead typing and calibration only; folding still needs task 12's own secondary rule and cooperative tertiary term.
  Say how you avoid inheriting that limitation.
